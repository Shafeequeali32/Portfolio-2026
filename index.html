Process

The 6-Step Design Workflow

01
Concept & Brief

Client provides sketches, reference imagery, stone dimensions, and desired metal specifications.

02
Design Development

Ergonomics, finger fit, proportions, and gemstone balance are mapped out carefully in 2D/3D sketches.

03
3D CAD Modelling

Precise NURBS construction in Rhino/MatrixGold, applying precise wall thickness and prong seats.

04
Technical Review

Verification of minimum casting wall thicknesses, shrinkage allowances, and stone security tolerances.

05
Photorealistic Renders

Studio rendering with KeyShot for client sign-off, marketing previews, and digital catalogues.

06
Final Delivery

Production STL files for 3D printing/casting, original 3DM CAD files, and high-res visualization assets.

Project Overview

Diamond Halo Ring

Cathedral micro-pave diamond halo ring with millimeter-exact stone setting geometry.

Category Fine Jewellery Ring
Service CAD + Photorealistic Rendering
Software Rhino 3D / KeyShot
Type Production Ready (STL + 3DM)

Engineering & Design Overview

This project involved developing a production-ready CAD master file for an international luxury bridal atelier. The center collet is engineered with four tapered prongs angled at 30° to firmly seat a 1.50ct round diamond without light obstruction. The surrounding micro-pave halo contains 24 stones with scallop bead walls and pre-drilled azimuth holes for effortless setting and ultrasonic cleaning fluid circulation.
